Teacher notes
  • Pick the week at the top. The words match the weekly Word Wall post in Google Classroom.
  • Review earlier weeks too mixes in words from weeks before the one you picked.
  • Cover the meanings lets students try to remember a meaning before they tap to check.
  • Wrong answers can be tried again, and a “Show me” button appears after two misses. Nothing is timed.
  • Some words can be more than one part of speech. The games use the part of speech listed on the Word Wall.
  • Nothing a student does is saved or sent anywhere. The page only remembers which week was picked on that device.
  • To open on a certain week by default, change DEFAULT_WEEK near the top of the script.
