Grow a Plant

Pick how much sun and water. Then press Grow.

💧 Water

Teacher note

Focus: plants need light and water. Have students predict first, then test different sun and water choices and compare the results.

Sink or Float

Look at the object. Guess if it will sink or float.

Teacher note

Focus: predict, test, check. After a round, ask what the floating things have in common.

Ice, Water, Steam

Slide to make it hot or cold. Watch the tiny dots.

ColdHot

Ice melts at 32°F. Water boils at 212°F.

Teacher note

Focus: the particle idea. In ice the particles are locked in place and shake. In water they slide. In steam they move fast and spread out. Hotter means faster.
